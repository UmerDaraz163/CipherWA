# CipherWA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

CipherWA is a browser extension that adds privacy and productivity tools to WhatsApp Web. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp LLC or Meta.

## The short version

CipherWA does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. It has no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything it stores stays in your browser.

## What the extension stores

| Data | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your settings (which switches are on, blur strength, default country code) | Chrome's synced extension storage | To remember your choices. If you use Chrome Sync, Google syncs these to your other browsers under your Google account. |
| Notes you write for a chat, with the chat's name and identifier | Chrome's local extension storage, on this device only | To show the right note when you open that chat |
| Canned replies you create | Chrome's local extension storage, on this device only | To offer them in the replies list |

## What the extension reads

To do its job, CipherWA reads parts of the WhatsApp Web page in your browser: the name of the open chat, the names in your chat list, and the member line of a group. It uses them to attach notes to the right chat and, when you click an export button, to create a CSV file that is saved to your computer. This content is processed inside your browser and is never sent anywhere.

CipherWA does not read or store the content of your messages.

## What the extension changes

When you turn the options on, CipherWA blurs parts of the page, and can stop WhatsApp Web from sending your typing indicator and read receipts. Phone numbers you type into "chat with an unsaved number" are passed to WhatsApp Web to open the chat and are not stored.

## Sharing

None. CipherWA makes no network requests of its own and has no third-party services.

## Your control

- Turn any feature off in the extension popup.
- Delete a note by clearing its text; delete a reply with the ✕ button.
- Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.

## Changes

If a future version changes what is stored or sent, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
